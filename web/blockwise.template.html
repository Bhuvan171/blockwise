Blockwise plans hotel and flight inventory for a B2B travel distributor. For every product and week it decides how many rooms or seats to prepay, how many to hold on a free-release allotment, and how many to buy only when an agent books, within one budget and at the risk you choose.

Prepaid block Allotment, free to hand back Bought as needed Expected demand

There are three ways to secure a room or a seat.

Each has a different price and a different risk. A forecast gives one number; the profit is in the mix, and the right mix changes by product, by week and with the budget.

    Does it work? We replayed history to find out.

    Each test steps through the past one decision at a time, using only what was known that day, then scores every plan against what really happened. The simple rules get the same forecast and the same safeguards.
