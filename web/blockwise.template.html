Assumptions and how to read this page

Prototype built for TBO VoyageHack 4.0 (Automate Inventory Optimization); not a TBO product. The portfolio is synthetic and every parameter is listed above. The proof uses the public Hotel booking demand dataset (António, de Almeida & Nunes, Data in Brief, 2019): real demand, booking dates, cancellations, prices and sold-out nights; contract rates are assumptions.
