The decision

Every room and seat can be bought three ways.

Months before travel, a distributor decides how to secure inventory. Each way has a different price and a different risk, and the right mix changes by product, by week and with the budget.

A forecast gives one number. The profit is in the mix: how many of each, for every product and week, inside one budget, with a clear view of the bad case.
